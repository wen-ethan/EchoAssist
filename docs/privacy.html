EchoAssist Privacy Policy

Support / Privacy Policy

Your conversations stay on your phone.
Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

EchoAssist has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything it hears and everything it writes down stays on your device. We, the people who made EchoAssist, never receive your audio, your transcripts, or any record of how you use the app.

Microphone and audio

When you tap Start, EchoAssist listens through the microphone and feeds the audio to two speech models running on your device: one turns speech into text, the other tells voices apart so each line can be labeled with a speaker.

Audio is processed as it arrives and is not written to storage. When you tap Stop, the audio is gone and only the text transcript is kept.

iOS asks for microphone permission the first time you start captions. You can revoke it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

What is stored on your device

  • Saved recordings: the title, date, speaker-labeled transcript, and summary of each session.
  • Your preferences: haptics, text size, appearance, and whether you've seen the onboarding walkthrough.
  • The downloaded speech models (about 800 MB), so captioning works without a network connection.
  • Translations you've generated, cached so switching languages is instant.

All of it lives in EchoAssist's own storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes your saved transcripts, protected by the same encryption as the rest of the backup.

AI summaries and translation

Summaries are written by Apple Intelligence's on-device model and translations by Apple's on-device Translation framework. Both run locally on your iPhone. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the summary is the opening of the transcript; no cloud service is used as a fallback.

The first time you pick a language, iOS may download that language pack from Apple. That request contains only the language you chose, never your transcript.

When EchoAssist uses the network

The app makes network requests in one case: downloading the two speech models from their public host the first time you set up captioning, or after you remove and re-download them. That request asks for model files and sends nothing about you or your recordings. Once the models are on your device, EchoAssist captions, summarizes, and translates fully offline.

Sharing is always your choice

Exporting a recording opens the standard iOS share sheet with the transcript as plain text. Where it goes from there is up to you, and that app's own privacy policy applies from that point.

Recording other people

EchoAssist captions whoever is speaking nearby, not just you. Laws about recording conversations vary by state and country, and some require everyone's consent. Please use EchoAssist responsibly and let people know when a conversation is being captioned.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single recording from its menu in the top-right corner.
  • Remove the speech models any time in Settings → Speech Models.
  • Delete the app to erase every transcript, summary, preference, and downloaded model at once.

Because nothing is ever uploaded, there is no copy of your data anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

EchoAssist doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
