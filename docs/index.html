EchoAssist

Support / Privacy Policy

EchoAssist gives live, speaker-labeled captions of nearby conversation, entirely on your iPhone.

Contact

Email wen.ethan@proton.me, or open an issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

I tap Start and nothing happens.

The speech models (about 800 MB) have to finish downloading before captions can start. Check Settings → Speech Models. If a download stopped partway, tap Resume Download.

Everything is labeled Speaker 1.

Speaker labels need more than one voice. With a single person talking, every line is correctly labeled Speaker 1. You can rename speakers inside a saved recording.

Why are some words lighter?

New words appear right away in a lighter shade, then darken once the app has confirmed who said them, usually within a second or two.

I don't see summaries.

Summaries use Apple Intelligence and need a device that supports it. On other devices, the summary shows the opening of the transcript instead.

Does EchoAssist upload my conversations?

No. Audio is never saved or uploaded, and transcripts stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
